# My Favorite Movie: Harry Potter

## About the Movie
**Harry Potter** is a fantasy movie series based on the books written by J.K. Rowling. It follows the life of a young wizard named Harry Potter as he discovers magic and faces different challenges.

## Main Characters
- Harry Potter
- Hermione Granger
- Ron Weasley
- Albus Dumbledore
- Severus Snape
  
## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because of its magical world and also, I want to go to the Hogwarts one day as a child. What makes this movie special to me is how it brings imagination to life. I want to experience the wizarding world and believed that it's real.
